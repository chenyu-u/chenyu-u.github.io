Projects

Selected Projects

Vertical-Axis Wind Turbine

University of Toronto Wind Turbine Design Team, 2025 to 2026

A six-foot turbine taken from computer-aided design and structural analysis through machining and assembly. I prototyped the actuator and shaft-braking subsystems, and in the wind tunnel it started delivering power at 4 m/s. The turbine you see spinning in the scene is this one.

Motorized Ski-Assist Device for Children

Client design project, 2026

A powered attachment so young cross-country skiers can keep up with their parents. From eighty concepts to two that I modelled and drew; my paddle-wheel design ranked first.

Pick-and-Place Robotic Arm

Concept with a working simulation
